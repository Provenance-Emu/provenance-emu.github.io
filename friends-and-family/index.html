Friends & Family Betas

Try early versions of Provenance, iFly, iCube, and RasterLab.

  1. Get TestFlight. Install Apple’s free TestFlight app on your iPhone or iPad.
  2. Unlock and choose an app. Open its invite, tap Accept, then Install.
  3. Using Apple TV? Accept on your iPhone or iPad first. On Apple TV, search the App Store for TestFlight, then open it with the same App Store account. Apps with an Apple TV beta will appear there, ready to install.
